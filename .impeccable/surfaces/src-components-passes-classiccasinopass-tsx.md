# Casino Pass 2.0 Beta

Mode: Experience. Audience: registered casino-night guests presenting a mobile pass at the door. Job: recognize the pass as a premium collectible, expose an unmistakable scan target, and reveal secondary event details without crowding the front. The current Casino Night pass remains available and unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A believable custom playing card becomes the credential itself. It refuses the category-default glowing digital ticket and treats restraint, print craft, and scan clarity as the spectacle.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory linen stock, burgundy and black letterpress, muted gold foil, a geometric spade-and-diamond border, fine double rules, softly rounded corners, and natural tabletop shadow. Controls are quiet typographic cues rather than floating app chrome.

STORY: The guest first sees their name, event identity, and a large protected QR. A slight tactile tilt confirms the card is interactive. Flipping reveals venue, access, raffle balance, fallback code, and pass reference; flipping again returns to the scan-ready face.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One proportional portrait card fills as much of the phone as possible without clipping. The front centers the event title and guest hierarchy above a large white QR bay in the lower third. The back uses the same border and stock, with a restrained information register and clear return cue. The QR never appears on the moving reverse face.

FORM: Classic Border, first of the refined Playing Card Reveal compositions and closest to the supplied physical-card reference. Surface concept seed: 6be4301b. Signature interaction: shallow pointer or device tilt with one deliberate spring flip on tap, click, Enter, or Space; reduced-motion users get an immediate state change.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: preserve the existing Casino Night theme, use real attendee/event data, maintain keyboard and reduced-motion access, and keep the front QR level, unobstructed, and high contrast.
